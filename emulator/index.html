furbulator web

The furbulator Furby Connect emulator by FurbLord, compiled to WebAssembly. It runs the toy's real firmware in your browser. Unofficial, not affiliated with Hasbro. furbulator is MIT licensed (license).

You need the Furby ROM pack (furby-connect.fcrom). Download it from the furbulator releases page, then pick it here. It stays on your computer: the page keeps a copy in your browser so you only pick it once.

Tools → Bluetooth console sends app commands. The Furby falls asleep after about a minute without interaction, like the real one: touch a sensor or shake it to wake it.

The % in the status bar is the speed. If your computer can't keep up, Emulation → Speed boost (Auto by default) lets the emulated chip do less spare work so it stays at full speed. The Chrome or Edge browsers are usually fastest.
